How these numbers are made

  • Meetings: verified physical meetings only, counted once per meeting ID, on Mon 7 – Sat 12 Sep and Mon 5 – Sat 10 Oct 2026. The date comes from meeting_datetime.
  • Executives: the 25 "New Executive" IDs in the seller file. Each meeting belongs to the employee_id on the meeting record.
  • Estimated distance between meetings: straight-line (Haversine) distance between consecutive meetings in start-time order, for each executive and day. There are no office legs, and days are never joined. It is not road distance and not GPS-tracked travel.
  • Excl. flagged: leaves out legs flagged as travel anomalies and legs touching a location anomaly. Use it for comparison only.
  • Clustering eligibility (October only): the "Eligible for clustering" value in the seller file for the GLID met. On October maps, blue = eligible and red = not eligible, and a dark ring marks a location more than 25 km from the office. September maps use green (normal) and red (more than 25 km away).
  • Missing coordinates: a meeting at a GLID with no seller coordinates is still counted, but it can't be mapped, and that day's route is marked incomplete.
